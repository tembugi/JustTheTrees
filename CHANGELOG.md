## v1.1.0

- The plan's buttons are the talent window's own: Apply Changes glows while the plan has unsaved changes, and the arrow next to it undoes them or clears the plan.
- Talents in the plan make the same sounds as in the talent window, and Apply Changes plays the talent window's apply animation.
- If something goes wrong, the addon turns itself off until you /reload, says so in chat, and leaves the talent window as the game draws it.
- Small fixes.
